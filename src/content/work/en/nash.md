### About the app

nash is a macOS app for drawing Nassi-Shneiderman diagrams (structograms). Lay out the logic of a program — sequences, branches, loops and procedures — before writing a single line of code. Whether you are learning to program or planning a tricky algorithm, nash keeps the structure clear.

### State of the app

**TestFlight beta**
nash is currently being tested via TestFlight. Public testing and the release on the Mac App Store are planned next.

### Features

- **Diagram editor** with drag and drop and Quick Insert (⌘K)
- **Hints** for incomplete diagrams
- **Procedures and calls** to structure larger programs
- **Zoom** for large diagrams
- **Export** to PDF, PNG and Markdown (pseudocode)
- **Quick Look** previews of `.nash` files in Finder
- **English and German**

### Privacy

nash works entirely offline. Your diagrams stay on your Mac, and the app collects no data. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/).
