This policy applies to the following apps by Appfrosch (Andreas Seeger):

- **[nash](/work/nash/)** (macOS)
- **[App-etizer](/work/app-etizer/)** (iOS, macOS)
- **[WhosCookin](/work/whoscookin/)** (iOS)

If an app is not listed here, it has its own privacy policy on its page.

## In short

These apps do not collect any data about you. There is no tracking, no analytics and no account. Nothing you create in the apps is sent to me. The one exception to "no advertising" is the ad banner in WhosCookin, described below.

## Your documents and settings

Everything you create in these apps stays on your device. Where an app syncs between your devices (App-etizer, WhosCookin), it does so through **your own iCloud account**. I have no access to that data; Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies to iCloud.

## Ads in WhosCookin

WhosCookin shows a small banner for other apps through [Kickstart Exchange](https://exchange.kickstart.tools), until you buy WhosCookin Plus. The banner only receives app-level details (such as the app version and App Store country), never your names, meals or plans, and it does not track you or your device. Details are in the [Kickstart Exchange privacy notice](https://exchange.kickstart.tools/privacy).

## TestFlight and the App Store

When you install an app through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple handles the download and, if applicable, the purchase. If you agree to share crash reports, diagnostics or TestFlight feedback with developers, Apple passes them on to me. I use them only to fix bugs and improve the app. You can turn sharing off at any time in your device's settings.

## Changes to this policy

If an app ever needs more than described here (for example a network service, an account or a third-party SDK), it gets its own privacy policy instead of a change to this one. Any other change to this page is reflected in the date below.
